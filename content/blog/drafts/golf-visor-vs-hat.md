# Golf Visor vs Hat: Which Is Better for Women?

Pick a visor if your hair is up or full, the day is hot, and you'll put **sunscreen on your part**. Pick a cap or bucket hat if the sun is strong, your scalp burns, or you want shade on your hair as well as your face.

That's the short answer to **golf visor vs hat**. The long answer depends on **your hair, the weather and your skin**, and plenty of us change our pick with the forecast.

## When is a visor the better choice?

A visor is the better choice on a hot day when your hair is up, full or freshly styled, and you'll put sunscreen on your part. [Druids](https://www.druids.com/blogs/golf/golf-cap-vs-visor) recommends a visor for hot weather, for golfers who like airflow, for anyone with enough hair to cover the scalp, and for anyone willing to put sunscreen on it. It also notes that visors are light and suit golfers with thick hair.

That describes a lot of us. A high pony, a bun, a puff, braids or locs worn up can sit where you put them, because a visor has no crown to press down on them.

Pick a visor when:

- The forecast is hot and you want air moving over the top of your head.
- Your hair is up high, in a bun, or full enough that a cap won't sit right.
- You'll put sunscreen on your part and hairline before you tee off, and again during the round.

## When is a cap the better choice?

A cap is the better choice when the sun is strong, your part or scalp burns, or your hair is short or thinning. Druids recommends a cap for strong sunlight and exposed courses, and says "golfers with short, thinning or no hair will usually get more reliable coverage from a cap."

A cap still leaves gaps. The [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/skin-cancer/sun-safety/index.html) says that if you wear a baseball cap, you should also cover your ears and the back of your neck with clothing, use sunscreen, or stay in the shade.

## Do visors protect you from the sun?

A visor shades your eyes and face, but it leaves your scalp, part and hairline open to the sun. The [Skin Cancer Foundation's golf advice](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/sun-protection-habits-golf-course/) calls caps and visors "good options which will stay in place and provide coverage to your face," because a wide-brimmed hat "isn't the most practical option on a golf course." Druids names the catch: "exposed areas such as the hairline and parting can still burn."

So a visor works best with sunscreen on the parts it leaves open. In a [Skin Cancer Foundation Ask the Expert answer](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/ask-the-expert-how-can-i-protect-my-scalp-better/), dermatologist Dendy Engelman, MD, says to apply sunscreen "when hair is dry to wherever your hair parts, any thinning or balding spots and all around the hairline." With braids or cornrows, that can mean several parts.

## What about a bucket hat?

A bucket hat gives you the widest shade of the three, because its brim runs all the way round. Druids says a bucket hat covers more of the face, ears and neck than a standard cap, though "the extra material may feel warmer than a visor." The CDC says a hat with "a brim all the way around that shades your face, your ears, and the back of your neck" gives the most protection.

## Why do so many women golfers wear visors?

[Glenmuir](https://www.glenmuir.com/news/first-golf-game-clothing-guide) says visors are "especially popular with female golfers." It doesn't say why, but the reasons are easy to see once you put one on:

- **Hair.** A visor has no crown, so nothing presses on the top of your hair or squeezes a high pony.
- **Airflow.** The open crown lets heat escape, Druids notes.
- **Look.** Druids says visors suit a lighter, more minimal feel.

The trade-off is the one above. Your part and hairline need sunscreen. Druids' answer is to own both, noting that "many golfers keep both in their golf bag and choose on the day."

## FAQ

### Is a visor or a cap cooler on a hot day?

A visor, in most cases. Its open crown lets heat escape, according to Druids. A cap with mesh panels at the sides and back gets more airflow than a solid one.

### Do I still need sunscreen if I wear a cap?

Yes. The Skin Cancer Foundation's golf advice is to "cover your ears and neck with sunscreen as these areas are still exposed." Our guide to [sun protection for darker skin](/journal/golf-sunscreen-darker-skin) on the course covers how much to use and when to reapply.

### Do women golfers wear visors?

Yes. Glenmuir calls visors "especially popular with female golfers," and they're the easy answer when your hair is up.

### What does UPF 50+ mean on a golf hat?

UPF is a rating of how much UV radiation a fabric lets through to your skin. The Skin Cancer Foundation rates UPF 50+ as "excellent." Our [explainer on what UPF 50+ means](/journal/upf-50-golf-hat-meaning) covers the rest.
