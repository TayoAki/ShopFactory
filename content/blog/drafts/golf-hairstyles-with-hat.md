# Golf Hairstyles That Work Under a Hat or Visor

The best **golf hairstyles with a hat** keep your hair out of the crown's way. Wear it low and through a ponytail opening, below the strap, or up and free under a visor. Our top pick is the simplest: a low ponytail through one of our [ponytail caps made for hair up](/collections/ponytail-caps).

A standard cap gives your hair two places to go: under the crown, or through the small gap above the strap. That's why the style matters as much as the hat.

Below are **nine golf hairstyles**, from ponytails to box braids, a puff, short hair and hair down. Each comes with a **best hat** and one thing to watch out for. Or jump straight to the [FAQ](#faq).

## 1. Low ponytail through a ponytail cap

Tie your ponytail low, put the cap on, and pull the ponytail through the opening at the back. It's the least fuss of the nine.

Want more than a plain pony? GOLF.com's [sectioned pony](https://golf.com/lifestyle/snazz-up-your-headwear-game-with-these-3-fun-ponytail-alternatives/?amp=1) starts the same way: a low ponytail, tied off again with an elastic "about a third of the way down."

**Best hat:** A cap with a concealed ponytail opening. We make four: the Front Nine Ponytail Cap, Range Day Mesh Cap, Cart Path Trucker and The Turn Cord Cap. All four list the opening for a ponytail.

**Watch out for:** Height. Tie the ponytail level with the opening, so the cap doesn't have to bend around it. Our [guide to ponytail hat designs](/journal/golf-hat-with-ponytail-hole) explains how each opening works.

## 2. High ponytail with an open-back cap or visor

A high ponytail sits higher on your head than a low one, so the hat has to make room for it up there.

**Best hat:** An open back or a visor. Our Back Nine Ponytail Cap has an open back panel with elastic cross bands, listed "for your ponytail or bun." A visor has no crown at all. GOLF.com's Jessica Marksbury makes the same point about pigtails: with a visor, ["you can opt for high pigtails, too."](https://golf.com/lifestyle/snazz-up-your-headwear-game-with-these-3-fun-ponytail-alternatives/?amp=1)

**Watch out for:** Our concealed-opening caps. We haven't tested a high ponytail in them, so we don't suggest them for this one. Under a visor, put sunscreen on your part.

## 3. Low bun at the nape

Gather a low ponytail at the nape of your neck. Twist it, wrap it around the base and pin it.

**Best hat:** The Back Nine's open back, which is listed for a bun, or a visor. If the bun sits fully below the strap, the cap doesn't touch it, so the hat matters less.

**Watch out for:** Height again. If the bun sits where the strap runs, the strap ends up on top of it. Keep it below the strap, or switch to the open back.

## 4. Two low braids

Part your hair down the middle and braid each side, starting low behind your ears. The braids hang below the cap's band on each side.

**Best hat:** Almost any cap. A low-profile cap like the Front Nine or the Tee Time Dad Cap keeps the crown close to your head.

[GOLF.com's ponytail alternatives piece](https://golf.com/lifestyle/snazz-up-your-headwear-game-with-these-3-fun-ponytail-alternatives/?amp=1) has a variation: double twisty buns. You make two low pigtails, twist each into a bun and pin them.

**Watch out for:** Where the braids start. Start them below the band, so the band sits on your head and not on the plaits.

## 5. Box braids or knotless braids, low bun or worn down

Box braids and knotless braids work under a hat two ways: down, or gathered into a low bun.

**Best hat:** Worn down, an unstructured, low-profile cap with the closure loosened, or a relaxed bucket hat. In a low bun, the Back Nine's open back or a visor.

**Watch out for:** The parts between your braids. A dermatologist writing for the Skin Cancer Foundation advises sunscreen, applied when your hair is dry, [to wherever your hair parts and all around the hairline](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/ask-the-expert-how-can-i-protect-my-scalp-better/). With braids, that's every part, and it matters most under a visor. This is general guidance, not medical advice. For more on fit, read our [guide to hats over braids and locs](/journal/golf-hat-with-braids-locs).

## 6. Half-up, half-down

Gather the top section of your hair and tie it at the back of your head. Leave the rest down. It keeps hair off your face when you look down at the ball.

**Best hat:** A visor leaves the top section free. With a ponytail cap, pull the top section's tail through the opening and let the rest hang below the band.

**Watch out for:** Tying the top section on your crown. That's exactly where a cap's crown goes. Tie it lower, at the back.

## 7. A puff with a visor

Gather your hair into a puff on top of your head or at the crown. A cap would sit right on it, so this is visor territory.

**Best hat:** A visor. Ours is the Clubhouse Visor: "Shade for your eyes, nothing on your hair." [Druids' cap vs visor guide](https://www.druids.com/blogs/golf/golf-cap-vs-visor) says golfers with thick hair may find visors especially comfortable, because the open top keeps hair from being pressed down. See our [golf visors for women](/collections/visors).

**Watch out for:** Your scalp. Druids warns that with a visor, "the hairline and parting can still burn." Put sunscreen on your hairline and part before you tee off. Our visor has no sun rating.

## 8. Short hair or a TWA under a low-profile cap

Short hair and a TWA (teeny weeny afro) need no special styling for a cap. The cap just has to fit.

**Best hat:** An unstructured, low-profile cap with a closure you can take in, like [a soft, low-profile dad cap](/products/tee-time-dad-cap). Our Tee Time Dad Cap is 100% cotton with a hideaway strap and an antique brass buckle. If most caps feel big, our Small Fit Performance Cap is made in a small fit for smaller heads, "so the brim sits where it should." Druids notes that golfers with short, thinning or no hair "will usually get more reliable coverage from a cap" than from a visor.

**Watch out for:** A cap that's too big. With little hair to fill the crown, a big cap can slide down over your eyes. If that's you, measure your head and read our guide to [golf hats for smaller heads](/journal/golf-hats-small-heads).

## How do I avoid hat hair after the round?

Hat hair comes from a crown pressing on your hair for a whole round. Styles that keep your hair out from under the crown give it less to press, and a visor skips the crown entirely. Pack a hair tie and a comb for lunch after.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest golf hairstyle with a hat?

A low ponytail through a ponytail cap. Tie it level with the opening, pull it through, and adjust the strap.

### What hairstyle works best with a golf visor?

Anything worn up: a high ponytail, high pigtails, a bun on top or a puff. A visor has no crown, so nothing sits on your hair. Put sunscreen on your part and hairline, because a visor leaves them in the sun.

### How do you wear a golf hat with long hair?

Keep it low. A low ponytail through a ponytail opening, a low bun below the strap or two low braids all stay out of the crown. Long hair worn down suits a bucket hat.

### Can you wear a golf hat with box braids?

Yes. Wear them down under an unstructured, low-profile cap with the closure loosened, or put them in a low bun with an open-back cap or a visor. Put sunscreen on the parts between braids.
