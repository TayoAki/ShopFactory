# Golf Hats With a Ponytail Hole: How to Choose One

A **golf hat with a ponytail hole** has an opening at the back, so your hair goes through the hat instead of bunching under it. There are three designs: a concealed opening, an open back with elastic cross bands, and a visor with no crown at all.

Which one works depends on **where you wear your hair**. A low pony, a high pony and a bun each ask something different of a hat.

Here's how to pick yours.

## What is a ponytail golf hat?

A ponytail golf hat is a cap with an opening at the back made for your hair. On a standard cap, the only gap at the back is the small arch above the strap. Your ponytail either squeezes through there or sits under the crown.

A ponytail cap gives your hair its own way out. Some caps hide the opening in the back panel. Others leave the back panel open.

Ponytail caps don't always make the big golf hat roundups. GOLF.com's [list of the best golf hats](https://golf.com/gear/accessories/best-golf-hats/) (July 2023) names nine hats, and one of them is labeled for women.

We make five [golf caps with a ponytail opening](/collections/ponytail-caps). Four have a concealed opening and one has an open back. The sections below explain how each design works, so you can pick by hairstyle.

## Which ponytail design suits your hair?

Pick by where your hair sits. A concealed opening is made for a ponytail. An open back with elastic cross bands is made for a ponytail or a bun. A visor has no crown, so nothing sits on your hair at all.

### Concealed ponytail opening

A concealed opening sits in the back panel of the cap, hidden in its design. With your hair up, your ponytail passes through it. With your hair down, you wear it like any other cap.

Four of our caps have one: the Front Nine Ponytail Cap, the Range Day Mesh Cap, the Cart Path Trucker and The Turn Cord Cap. All four list the opening for a ponytail. None of them lists it for a bun, so we won't either.

We also haven't tested a high ponytail in these openings. Until we do, we point a high pony to the open back or a visor.

### Open back with elastic cross bands

This design leaves the back panel open, with elastic bands crossing the gap. Your ponytail or bun sits in the opening.

Our [open-back cap with elastic cross bands](/products/back-nine-ponytail-cap) is the Back Nine Ponytail Cap. Its product details name both styles: "an open back with elastic cross bands for your ponytail or bun." It is made with fabric rated UPF 50+.

### A visor (no crown at all)

A visor is a brim on a band. There's no crown, so a high pony, a bun or a puff stays free. Our Clubhouse Visor's description says it plainly: "Shade for your eyes, nothing on your hair."

The trade-off is your scalp. [Druids' cap vs visor guide](https://www.druids.com/blogs/golf/golf-cap-vs-visor) notes that a visor leaves the scalp exposed, and "the hairline and parting can still burn." We don't give our visor a sun rating.

If you go with a visor, put sunscreen on your part and hairline. A dermatologist writing for the Skin Cancer Foundation advises applying it [when your hair is dry, to wherever your hair parts and all around the hairline](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/ask-the-expert-how-can-i-protect-my-scalp-better/). That's general guidance, not medical advice.

## Can you wear a ponytail cap with a bun?

Yes, if the cap has an open back. The Back Nine Ponytail Cap is listed "for your ponytail or bun." Our four concealed-opening caps are listed for ponytails only, so for a bun we'd pick the open back or a visor.

Where the bun sits matters too. A bun on top of your head sits right where a cap's crown goes, so a visor is the simplest answer there. For a bun at the back of your head, the Back Nine's open back is the one we list for it.

## What else to check: profile, structure, closure and sweatband

The opening is half the fit. Crown height, how stiff the front is, how the strap adjusts and what's inside the band decide how the cap feels by the back nine.

- **Profile is crown height.** A low profile sits closer to your head. A mid profile stands taller. The Front Nine, Range Day, Back Nine and Turn Cord are low profile. The Cart Path Trucker is mid profile.
- **Structure is how stiff the front is.** An unstructured cap has a soft front that settles onto your head. A structured front holds its own shape. The Front Nine, Range Day and Back Nine are unstructured. The Turn Cord is light-structured, and the Cart Path Trucker is structured.
- **Closures adjust in steps or anywhere along the strap.** A snapback clicks into set holes. A tri-glide or hook-and-loop strap can stop at any point. The Front Nine has an antique brass tri-glide and the Back Nine a micro hook-and-loop. The Range Day has a plastic snapback, and the Cart Path and Turn Cord have snapbacks.
- **Every concealed-opening cap has a makeup-resistant sweatband.** All four bands are also breathable. The Front Nine and Turn Cord bands wick moisture, and the Range Day's manages it. ## Ponytail cap or visor: which is better in the heat?

A visor lets more heat out. [Druids](https://www.druids.com/blogs/golf/golf-cap-vs-visor) says its open crown "allows heat to escape from the top of your head." It adds that a visor is "only a sensible option when the exposed scalp is properly protected." So in the heat, a visor works if sunscreen goes on your part and hairline first.

A cap shades the top of your head, your forehead and your eyes. Your ears, the sides of your face and the back of your neck still need sunscreen, Druids adds. The Skin Cancer Foundation calls baseball caps and visors ["good options which will stay in place and provide coverage to your face"](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/sun-protection-habits-golf-course/). For a cap that breathes, look at the mesh backs above. Our [full visor vs hat comparison](/journal/golf-visor-vs-hat) goes deeper.

## FAQ

### Can I wear a ponytail cap with my hair down?

Yes. A concealed opening hides in the back of the cap, so with your hair down you wear it like any cap. The Back Nine's open back stays open, and your hair falls over it.

### Do ponytail caps work for small heads?

Our one small-fit cap, the Small Fit Performance Cap, has no ponytail opening. Of our ponytail caps, the Front Nine, Range Day and Back Nine are unstructured and low profile. The Front Nine and Back Nine straps also stop at any point along their length. Measure first. Our guide on [what to look for if caps feel big](/journal/golf-hats-small-heads) walks through it.
