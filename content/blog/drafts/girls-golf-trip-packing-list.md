# Girls' Golf Trip Packing List: What to Bring for Three Days

A **girls' golf trip packing list** for three days covers one outfit per round, a hat for each kind of weather, a sun kit, travel gear and something for dinner. Pack to the list below and you can skip the third bag.

## What should I pack for a girls' golf trip?

For three days and three rounds, pack three golf outfits, one warm layer, golf shoes, two to four hats, a sun kit, your clubs and two dinner looks. Here's the full list.

| Category | What to pack | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Golf outfits | 3 tops, 2 bottoms | One outfit per round; the pieces mix |
| Layer | 1 quarter-zip or light jacket | Early tee times and flights run cool |
| Shoes | Golf shoes, plus 1 pair for off the course | Break new golf shoes in before the trip |
| Hats | 2-4 | One per kind of weather, plus one for dinner |
| Sun kit | SPF 30+ sunscreen, sunscreen stick, sunglasses | Reapply every two hours |
| Small gear | 2 gloves, balls, tees, ball marker, divot tool, towel | A spare glove for when one gets damp |
| Rain | Umbrella or rain jacket | Check the forecast the night before you pack |
| Clubs | Clubs in a travel bag, or rentals booked | Check your airline's rules and fees |
| Dinner | 2 outfits, 1 pair of shoes | One that works for the 19th hole |
| Travel | ID, chargers, a small bag for the cart | Phone, sunscreen and snacks go in the small bag |
| For the group | Matching hats | Order well before the trip |

## One outfit per round, plus a capsule

Three rounds means three golf outfits, not three of everything. Build a small capsule so every top works with every bottom:

- **Two bottoms.** A skort and a pair of pants or shorts, in neutral colors.
- **Three tops.** Colors that go with both bottoms, so you can swap on the fly.
- **One layer.** It covers the cold first tee and the flight home.
- **Socks for every round.** Plus one spare pair for the day it rains.
- **Sun-friendly pieces.** The Skin Cancer Foundation says long sleeves and long pants "provide the greatest coverage" ([SCF golf advice](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/sun-protection-habits-golf-course/)).

## Your sun kit

Three rounds in a row is a lot of sun. The Skin Cancer Foundation's [golf advice](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/sun-protection-habits-golf-course/) covers what to pack:

- **Sunscreen.** At least one ounce of water-resistant, broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher on exposed skin, 30 minutes before you play.
- **A reapply plan.** Reapply every two hours, or more often if you're sweating heavily. The SCF suggests picking a hole or two as your reapply stop.
- **Ears and neck.** A cap leaves them exposed, so they get sunscreen too.
- **Sunglasses.** Choose a pair that blocks 99-100% of UV radiation.
- **Tee times.** Book early morning or late afternoon, when the sun is less intense.

At an ounce per application, one four-hour round with a reapply uses about two ounces. Three rounds add up, so bring more than a mini tube. This is general guidance, not medical advice. For tinted options and darker skin, see [golf sun protection for darker skin](/journal/golf-sunscreen-darker-skin).

## Check each course's dress code

Dress codes vary from club to club, as [Golf Monthly](https://www.golfmonthly.com/features/im-a-long-time-golf-club-member-and-the-dress-code-still-confuses-me) puts it. A trip with three courses can mean three sets of rules.

Before you pack, check each course's website or call its pro shop. Ask about collars, denim, shorts length and what's expected in the clubhouse. Hats indoors have their own customs, covered in our [golf hat etiquette guide](/journal/golf-hat-etiquette).

## Travel gear

The non-golf extras that make a golf weekend run smoothly:

- **A travel bag for your clubs**, padded or hard-sided. If you'd rather not fly with clubs, ask each course about rentals before you go.
- **A small bag for the cart** with your phone, sunscreen stick and snacks.
- **Chargers and a power bank** for your phone and any rangefinder.
- **A luggage tag** on your golf bag.
- **A laundry bag** for damp gloves and sweaty layers.
- **A refillable water bottle.**
- **Every tee time, saved or printed**, with each course's phone number, in case you're running late.

## Golf trip packing FAQ

### How many hats should I pack for a golf weekend?

Two to four. Bring one for each kind of weather you expect, plus one that works at dinner.

### Should I bring my own clubs on a golf trip?

If you're driving, yes. If you're flying, compare your airline's fees with the course's rental clubs, and book rentals ahead.

### What do you wear to dinner on a golf trip?

Something that works for the restaurant, plus a layer for the walk back. A rope cap fits the 19th hole if you head straight there from the course.
