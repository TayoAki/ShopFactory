# Gifts for Women Golfers: 25 Ideas She'll Actually Use

Good **gifts for women golfers** are things she'll use every round: a hat that fits her head and her hair, sunscreen she'll reapply, a lesson she keeps putting off. Below are 25 ideas, grouped by budget, from small add-ons to a whole trip.

Most gift guides lead with gadgets. The gift people get stuck on is the one she'd wear, because **you don't know her size**. We'll fix that too.

## Quick picks by how well you know her golf

Budget is one filter. How much you know about her game is the other.

- **You know her ball, her glove size and her home course.** Go specific: a dozen of her ball, a glove, a range card or lessons at that course.
- **You know she plays, but not much else.** Pick things that don't need a size or a brand: a towel, socks, a sunscreen stick, a tumbler, or a hat with a free fit exchange.
- **She's brand new.** Give her a way in: a lesson package, a beginner clinic, an LPGA Amateurs membership, and ball markers to go with her first round.
- **You're buying for the whole group.** Matching hats, a tee time for four, or lunch after the round.

## Gifts under $25

Small things she goes through fast. These budgets are rough, since prices vary by brand and store.

### 1. Ball markers

A ball marker goes right behind her ball on the green before she lifts it. The Rules accept "a tee, a coin, an object made to be a ball-marker" ([R&A definitions](https://www.randa.org/en/rog/definitions)), so a nicer one is a pure upgrade. If she's new, it's one of the [golf terms beginners hear first](/journal/golf-terms-for-beginners).

### 2. Tees

She'll lose them. Everyone does. A bag of tees in a color she likes will be gone by spring. The Rules cap a tee at four inches, so skip the novelty giants.

### 3. A divot tool

A small fork for fixing the mark her ball leaves when it lands on the green. It lives in her pocket and comes out on most greens.

### 4. A sunscreen stick

Four hours outside adds up. The Skin Cancer Foundation's [golf advice](https://www.skincancer.org/blog/sun-protection-habits-golf-course/) is water-resistant, broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher, reapplied every two hours or more often if she's sweating heavily. It suggests a sport formula "which is sweat resistant and won't run into your eyes." A stick fits in a bag pocket for the reapply on the 9th tee. (General guidance, not medical advice. For darker skin, see our guide to [golf sun protection for darker skin](/journal/golf-sunscreen-darker-skin).)

### 5. A golf towel

Clubs, balls and hands all get dirty. She'll reach for a towel that clips to her bag on every hole. Pick one in a color that hides grass stains, or one that matches her bag.

### 6. Golf socks

Low, cushioned and in her colors. The only size you need is her shoe size.

Our Clubhouse Visor is "shade for your eyes, nothing on your hair," which suits a high ponytail, a bun or braids. It makes no UPF claim, and her part and scalp stay in the sun, so pair it with that sunscreen stick.

### 10. A golf glove

Only if you know her size. Check the size on the glove already in her bag. Right-handed golfers usually wear one glove, on the left hand.

### 11. A dozen of the ball she already plays

Yes, balls. The trick is buying her ball, not yours. Check the brand and model in her bag first.

### 12. An insulated tumbler

Cold water on the 14th hole beats a warm plastic bottle. Pick one that fits her cart's cup holder.

## Bigger gifts

Prices for these vary by course, pro and store, so ask before you budget.

### 13. Golf lessons

A package of lessons with a teaching pro. Ask the course she plays which packages it sells. If she'd rather learn in a group, several of the [Black women's golf groups we checked](/journal/black-women-golf-groups) run beginner clinics.

### 14. A range card

A prepaid card for range balls at the course where she practices. She'll use it on weeknights after work, and it takes away the "is it worth a trip to the range" question.

### 15. A club fitting

A fitter watches her swing and suggests clubs that suit it. It's a good gift for a golfer still playing a borrowed or starter set. Book the appointment and let her choose the clubs after.

### 16. A golf bag

If she's carrying a hand-me-down, a bag she picks herself is a big upgrade. A stand bag has legs and suits walking; a cart bag rides on a golf cart. Bags are personal, so let her choose.

### 17. A rangefinder

A device that tells her the distance to the flag. A laser model measures to whatever she points it at, and a GPS model reads from a course map. Ask her golf friends which kind the group uses.

## Experience gifts

For the woman who has everything, give her time on the course.

### 18. An LPGA Amateurs membership

The LPGA Amateur Golf Association has local chapters for golf, networking and social events, and members must be 18 or older. Its memberships include access to its Let's Go Golf 101 program, and the [memberships page](https://lpgaamateurs.com/memberships/) has a "Gift of Membership" option. ### 19. A round at the course she keeps talking about

Book a tee time for two and go with her. It's the gift and the plan in one. Add the cart fee and lunch after, and she won't reach for her wallet once.

Hitting balls with food and friends, indoors or under lights. Good for winter, and for the friend who's still deciding if golf is for her.

## Golf gift FAQ

### What do you get a woman golfer who has everything?

Time on the course. A lesson package, a round at a course she loves, an LPGA Amateurs membership or a weekend trip all beat another gadget.

### What's a good golf gift under $25?

Ball markers, tees, a divot tool, a sunscreen stick, a towel or golf socks. She'll use every one of them.

### What's a good Christmas gift for a woman golfer?

For winter, a beanie, a range card or a simulator night keeps her swinging until spring. If you're ordering a hat, order early, since ours are made to order.

### What's a good golf gift for a mom who just started playing?

Something that helps her keep going: a lesson package, a beginner clinic, or a round with you. Add ball markers and a towel for her first bag, and a hat that adjusts.

### Is a golf hat a good gift if I don't know her size?

Yes, if it adjusts and the exchange is free. Ours come with free fit exchanges within 30 days of delivery.
