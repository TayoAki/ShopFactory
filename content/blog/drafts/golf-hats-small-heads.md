# Golf Hats for Women With Small Heads: What Actually Fits

For a small head, look for [a cap made in a small fit](/products/small-fit-performance-cap), or a **low-profile, unstructured cap** with a closure that cinches. **Measure your head first**, so you know where you sit before you buy.

A cap that's too big slides toward your eyes, and a tall crown stands up off your head. On the first tee, that's the last thing you want to be fixing.

## Why are golf hats too big for me?

A one-size cap has to cover a wide range of heads. A small head sits at the bottom of that range, or below it. A tall crown and a stiff front make the gap more obvious.

One golfer on the Golf Monthly forum put it this way: ["everything I've tried in AG or local pro shops are HUGE"](https://forums.golfmonthly.com/threads/golf-hats.106360/). Here is why that happens.

**One-size ranges have a floor.** [Nike's hat sizing guide](https://www.nike.com/sg/a/how-to-measure-hat-size) says "one-size hats generally fit head measurements from 54 centimetres to 61 centimetres." That's about 21.3 to 24 inches. The same guide calls a 56 cm head (about 22 inches) a hat size 7, "small to medium." So a head that's only small to medium sits 2 cm from the bottom of that range. A small head can sit right at the edge or under it.

**The strap only takes in the back.** Tightening the strap shrinks the band at the back of the cap. It doesn't make the crown any shorter, so a deep cap can still sit low on your forehead.

**Tall crowns stay tall.** Profile is the height of the crown. A high-profile crown keeps its height whatever size your head is, so on a small head there's more cap above you.

**Structured fronts keep their shape.** A structured cap has a stiffened front that holds its form. It won't soften and settle onto a smaller head the way an unstructured front does.

## How do I measure my head for a golf hat?

Wrap a soft tape around your head, about 2.5 cm (1 inch) above your eyebrows and just above your ears, and read the number where the tape meets. It takes under a minute.

1. **Wrap.** Run a soft tape around your head, just above your ears and across your forehead. Nike's guide places it "about 2.5cm above your eyebrows" and "just below the small knot at the back of your skull."
2. **Read.** Note the number where the tape meets. Our fit guide works in inches; Nike's works in centimeters. One inch is 2.54 cm.
3. **Match.** Compare your number with the size range a brand lists. If hats often feel big on you, start with a small-fit cap. Otherwise, choose a cap that adjusts.

No tape? Use a phone charger cable, then lay it against a ruler. Nike suggests a piece of string the same way.

Measure with your hair the way you'll wear it to play. Braids, locs or a bun under the band all add to the number.

### Low profile and unstructured

Low profile means a shorter crown, so less cap stands above your head. Unstructured means a soft front with no stiffening, so the cap settles onto your head instead of holding its own shape.

Put the two together and a standard-size cap has a better chance on a smaller head. Five of our caps are unstructured and low profile: the Small Fit, Front Nine, Range Day, Tee Time and Back Nine.

### Closures that cinch: hook-and-loop, D-ring, tri-glide, hideaway strap, snapback

The closure decides how much a cap can take in, and in what steps.

- **Hook-and-loop** strips grip wherever they meet, so you can stop at any point. The Back Nine has a micro hook-and-loop closure, and so does the Clubhouse Visor.
- **A D-ring** closure runs the strap through a ring and fastens it back on itself. The Small Fit cap pairs a D-ring with micro hook-and-loop.
- **A tri-glide** is a three-bar slider. The strap runs through it, and you slide it to any point. The Front Nine has an antique brass tri-glide.
- **A hideaway strap** is a fabric strap that runs through a buckle, with the tail tucked away. The Tee Time Dad Cap's buckle is antique brass.
- **A snapback** clicks into a row of holes, so it adjusts in steps rather than at any point. The Range Day, Cart Path Trucker, Turn Cord and Sunwashed Rope Cap close with snapbacks.

How far any closure goes depends on the length of its strap. The closure type alone won't tell you whether a cap gets small enough, so measure first.

## Which hat styles tend to sit high?

Caps with a tall, structured crown, and one-size hats with no strap, are the ones to check closely. None of this means a hat won't fit you. It tells you what to read on the product page before you buy.

- **High-profile, structured crowns.** Check the profile and structure lines first. Ours list both: the Sunwashed Rope Cap is semi-structured with a high profile, and the Cart Path Trucker is structured with a mid profile.
- **One-size buckets with no strap.** A bucket with no adjuster fits the way it's made. Neither of ours lists an adjuster. The 19th Hole Bucket is a relaxed fit, and the Weekend Bucket Hat is a snug fit, one size fits all.
- **Beanies are the exception.** Nike's guide notes that "beanies tend to stretch, which makes them suited for a range of head sizes." Our Winter Rules Beanie is one size, in a stretchy knit.

## FAQ

### What counts as a small head?

There's no official cut-off. Nike's sizing guide says one-size hats generally fit heads from 54 to 61 cm, and calls a 56 cm head a hat size 7, "small to medium." If you measure near 54 cm (about 21.3 inches) or under, you're at or below the bottom of that range.

### Are youth hats a good option?

They can be, if the size range matches your tape number. Youth caps are sized for kids, so check the listed measurement, crown height and brim on the product page before you buy. Style is the other question. A youth cap may not match the rest of your golf outfit.

### Do ponytail caps come in a small fit?

Our one small-fit cap has no ponytail opening. The Front Nine and Back Nine are low-profile, unstructured ponytail caps with straps that stop at any point. Our guide to [choosing a golf hat with a ponytail hole](/journal/golf-hat-with-ponytail-hole) compares all five.

### Should I measure with my hair up or down?

Measure the way you'll wear your hair on the course. If braids, locs or a low bun sit under the band, they're part of what the cap has to go around. For more on fitting a cap over a lot of hair, see our guide to [wearing a golf hat with braids or locs](/journal/golf-hat-with-braids-locs).

### Is a visor better for a small head?

A visor has no crown, so crown height stops mattering. The band still has to fit, and our Clubhouse Visor adjusts with hook-and-loop. A visor also leaves your scalp in the sun, so put sunscreen on your part.
